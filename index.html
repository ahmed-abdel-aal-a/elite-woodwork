01 • About Us

SAUDI CRAFTSMANSHIP GLOBAL DISTINCTION

Headquartered in Riyadh, ELITE has evolved into the most trusted name in bespoke architectural woodwork, luxury fit-outs, and handcrafted contract furniture. As a purely Saudi enterprise, we combine ancestral carpentry mastery with European CNC precision engineering.

With over 15 years of industry leadership, we translate the world's most intricate architectural blueprints into tactile reality. Our approach is deeply committed to sustainable forestry, certified timber chains, and green manufacturing inside the Kingdom.
